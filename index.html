🔍 What Is txfeecalc?

Every time you move cryptocurrency on the Ethereum network, you pay a fee. That fee is called "gas," and it changes constantly depending on how busy the network is. Most people only discover the price after they have already clicked send — and by then it is too late.

txfeecalc solves that problem. It is a small, self-contained program for Windows that lets you sit down at your computer, type in a few numbers, and instantly see the real cost of a transaction in both ETH and your everyday currency. You can compare normal transfers against token swaps, test different fee speeds, and decide whether it is a good moment to send or better to wait.

Because the app runs entirely on your own machine, you never hand over a wallet address, a private key, or any personal information. There is no login screen, no subscription, and no telemetry. It simply does the arithmetic you would otherwise do on a scrap of paper — quickly, accurately, and every single time.

The project is written in C++ using the Windows desktop interface and is published as open source, meaning anyone can inspect how it works, learn from it, or contribute improvements.

🧭 How to Use txfeecalc

The screen is deliberately uncluttered. You will see a handful of input boxes and a result area. Here is what each part does:

Gas limit — the maximum amount of gas your transaction is allowed to use. A simple transfer is usually 21,000. A token transfer or a swap needs more.

Gas price in gwei — the price you are willing to pay per unit of gas. Your wallet or a block explorer will show the current figure.

ETH price — the current value of one Ethereum in your local currency, used to convert the result into familiar money.

Result panel — shows the total fee in ETH and in your currency, updated as you type.

A typical session looks like this:

  1. Pick a transaction preset, or type your own gas limit.
  2. Enter the current gwei price from your wallet or explorer.
  3. Type in the ETH price so the app can convert the result.
  4. Read the total cost and compare it against the different speed settings.
  5. Adjust the numbers until you find a fee you are comfortable paying, then place the trade in your own wallet.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The app is a calculator, not a wallet, so it will never ask for a seed phrase or private key.
